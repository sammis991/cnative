### Q2. Fill in the Blanks

(i) **Home** row — this is the resting position of the fingers while touch typing. The index fingers rest on **F** and **J**, which have raised bumps.

(ii) **Operating System (OS)** — e.g., Windows, Ubuntu (Linux), macOS. It manages hardware, files, memory and processes.

(iii) **Business Process Outsourcing** — the practice of hiring an outside company/agency to handle business operations such as customer support, payroll or data entry.

(iv) **Cell** — each cell has a unique address made of the column letter followed by the row number (e.g., B4).

## SECTION – B

### Q4. (2 marks)
**Communication** is the process of exchanging information, ideas, thoughts, feelings and messages between two or more people through a commonly understood medium, so that the message is received and understood as intended.

**Two methods/types of communication:**
1. **Verbal communication** – use of words, either spoken (face-to-face talk, telephone) or written (letters, e-mails, SMS).
2. **Non-verbal communication** – use of body language such as gestures, facial expressions, posture and eye contact.
*(Visual communication – signs, charts, posters – is a third type.)*

### Q5. (2 marks)
**Self-management** (also called self-control) means understanding oneself and being able to manage one’s own emotions, thoughts, time and behaviour in order to achieve personal and professional goals independently.

**Two benefits:**
1. It helps a person to set and achieve goals, complete work on time and increase productivity.
2. It builds self-confidence, reduces stress and improves relationships with others.

### Q6. (2 marks)

## SECTION – C

### Q8. (3 marks) — Qualities of a Successful Entrepreneur *(any three)*

1. **Hard working & Patient:** An entrepreneur works long hours with full dedication and does not expect instant results. Success in business takes time and continuous effort.
2. **Confident & Risk-taking:** An entrepreneur believes in his/her idea and is willing to take calculated risks, such as investing money in a new product without any guarantee of profit.
3. **Creative and Innovative:** An entrepreneur constantly thinks of new ideas, better products or improved ways of doing things to meet customer needs.
4. **Good Decision-maker & Leader:** Able to take quick, correct decisions and to motivate, guide and manage a team.
5. **Responsible & Honest:** Takes responsibility for his/her actions and deals fairly with customers, employees and society.

- Memorise the **7 C’s**: Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, Courteous.
- Remember file extensions: **Writer .odt | Calc .ods | Impress .odp | Base .odb**
- Common shortcuts: Ctrl+C (Copy), Ctrl+X (Cut), Ctrl+V (Paste), Ctrl+Z (Undo), Ctrl+Y (Redo), Ctrl+S (Save), Ctrl+P (Print), Ctrl+H (Find & Replace), F7 (Spell check), F5 (Slide show).
